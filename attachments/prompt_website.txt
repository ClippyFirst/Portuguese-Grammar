# MASTER PROMPT — Portuguese Grammar Reference for Ukrainian Speakers

You are the lead software architect, UX/UI designer, frontend engineer and technical product designer.

Your task is to build a modern, fast, static-first grammar reference website dedicated initially to PORTUGUESE GRAMMAR FOR UKRAINIAN-SPEAKING LEARNERS.

The website is NOT a language course.

It is NOT a vocabulary trainer.

It is NOT a flashcard application.

It is NOT primarily organized by CEFR levels.

It is a comprehensive grammar REFERENCE: a place where a Ukrainian-speaking learner can quickly find a grammatical topic, understand it deeply, see clear formulas and examples, understand exceptions and common mistakes, compare major Portuguese varieties, and navigate to related topics.

The core UX principle is:

"Find → understand → compare → remember."

The website should preserve the strongest ideas of high-quality grammar reference books while substantially improving information architecture, search, explanations, examples, error correction, regional/standard variation and navigation.

==================================================
1. TARGET AUDIENCE
==================================================

Primary audience:

Ukrainian-speaking people learning Portuguese.

The reference must be designed specifically for Ukrainian-speaking learners.

The learner may be interested in:

- Brazilian Portuguese (PT-BR);
- European Portuguese (PT-PT);
- African varieties of Portuguese;
- Portuguese in other Lusophone regions.

Brazilian and European Portuguese should be treated as the two principal reference varieties.

The site must NOT assume that "Portuguese" automatically means Brazilian Portuguese or European Portuguese.

Where relevant, explicitly distinguish:

- Brazilian Portuguese;
- European Portuguese;
- African Portuguese varieties;
- other Lusophone usage.

However, do not overwhelm ordinary grammar pages with unnecessary regional information.

The site must be understandable specifically to Ukrainian speakers.

Therefore explanations must:

- be written in natural Ukrainian;
- explicitly explain differences between Ukrainian and Portuguese where useful;
- anticipate errors caused by Ukrainian language structure;
- avoid explanations that assume English as the learner's native language;
- use English terminology only when useful for international terminology/SEO;
- never make English the hidden pedagogical reference language;
- clearly distinguish Portuguese grammatical concepts from superficially similar Ukrainian concepts.

The user should be able to understand:

"Why does Portuguese work this way?"

rather than merely memorize:

"Portuguese uses this form."

Only include false friends when they illuminate a grammatical or structural issue.

==================================================
40. REGIONAL VARIATION
==================================================

Portuguese is pluricentric.

Do not present:

"Brazilian Portuguese = Portuguese"

or:

"European Portuguese = Portuguese"

as the only perspective.

Where relevant distinguish:

- Brazil;
- Portugal;
- Angola;
- Mozambique;
- Cape Verde;
- Guinea-Bissau;
- São Tomé and Príncipe;
- Timor-Leste;
- other Lusophone regions where reliable grammatical information exists.

Regional information should be concise on ordinary pages.

Create deeper dedicated regional pages where necessary.

Always distinguish:

- standard;
- formal;
- colloquial;
- regional;
- emerging/local;
- prescriptive recommendations.

==================================================
41. CONTENT DEPTH
==================================================

The site must be deep.

"Deep" does NOT mean unnecessarily verbose.

Each topic should answer:

- What is it?
- How is it formed?
- When is it used?
- When is it NOT used?
- What are the exceptions?
- What are the common mistakes?
- What does a Ukrainian speaker usually misunderstand?
- What are the important PT-BR/PT-PT differences?
- Are there relevant African/Lusophone differences?
- What are the related grammatical concepts?
- What terminology might the learner encounter in other grammar books?

For major topics, provide enough examples to demonstrate the rule rather than merely state it.

==================================================
47. CONTENT QUALITY CONTROL
==================================================

Never generate filler.

Never repeat the same explanation using different wording just to increase length.

Never invent grammatical rules.

Distinguish:

- standard grammar;
- colloquial usage;
- regional usage;
- prescriptive recommendations;
- disputed/variable usage;
- historical usage when relevant.

For difficult topics, prefer authoritative grammatical consensus.

When Brazilian and European Portuguese differ, do not simplify the difference into:

"Brazil says X, Portugal says Y"

unless the distinction is genuinely systematic.

Explain:

- frequency;
- register;
- optionality;
- context;
- regional distribution.

==================================================
48. SOURCING AND AUTHORITY
==================================================

For difficult or disputed grammar topics, prioritize reliable linguistic sources.

Where possible base explanations on:

- authoritative Portuguese grammars;
- major Portuguese and Brazilian linguistic references;
- official orthographic standards;
- academic sources;
- established dictionaries and reference works.

Do not rely on random language-learning blogs as primary authority.

The site may simplify explanations for learners, but simplification must not distort the grammatical facts.
